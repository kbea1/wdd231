Discover Peru Trails

Individual Project Site Plan by Karla Loyaga

Site Name

Discover Peru Trails. This name invites visitors to explore routes and places across all of Peru, from the coast to the Andes and the Amazon.

Site Purpose

The site helps travelers plan a trip to Peru. It provides:

  • Information about popular destinations in each region.
  • Recommended routes and travel tips.
  • A contact form for visitors who want to ask questions or request trip suggestions.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Peru for a first-time traveler?
  • How many days do I need to visit Cusco and Machu Picchu, and what is the best time of year to go?
  • Which beaches and archaeological sites can I visit on the northern coast, near Trujillo?
